# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Product Managers to understand acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Collaborate with QA/Testing Lead on test coverage and quality standards
- Report technical risks and blockers to Technical Leads and Project Managers
- Receive guidance on design decisions from Technical Leads/Architects

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Project Managers on delivery schedules and resource planning
- Collaborate with Product Leads on strategic alignment and prioritization
- Work with Developers on scope trade-offs and technical feasibility
- Engage with QA/Testing Leads on quality acceptance criteria

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Product Managers on prioritization and roadmap alignment
- Escalate risks and blockers to Product Leads and Sponsors
- Coordinate with Scrum Masters to manage team workflow and velocity
- Communicate project status to Sponsors and stakeholders

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic oversight and guidance for product initiatives. They review and approve project charters, align initiatives with business strategy, and serve as escalation points for major decisions. They bridge product strategy with delivery execution.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve project one-pagers and business cases
- Ensure alignment between project initiatives and business strategy
- Escalate and resolve critical blockers and conflicts between teams
- Validate that success metrics align with business objectives
- Guide prioritization decisions between competing initiatives
- Approve go/no-go decisions at key project gates

### Goals
- Ensure strategic alignment of all project investments
- Reduce friction in cross-team collaboration
- Maximize business impact of product initiatives
- Enable data-informed decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Project charter reviews and approvals
- Weekly alignment with Product Managers
- Escalation discussions with Sponsors for business-critical decisions
- Quarterly strategy reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Project Managers on project approval and gate reviews
- Partner with Product Managers on roadmap prioritization
- Provide guidance to Technical Leads on strategic technical decisions
- Communicate outcomes and learnings to Sponsors

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategy, ensuring that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They collaborate with the development team to identify test coverage gaps and advocate for reliability and user satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy and test plans for projects
- Validate acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Plan and execute manual and automated testing
- Identify and triage quality issues and risks
- Contribute to Definition of Done discussions
- Prepare smoke tests and post-deployment verifications
- Advise on test coverage and quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal critical defects
- Reduce time spent in QA cycles without sacrificing quality
- Maintain clear communication between quality concerns and delivery teams
- Support continuous improvement in testing practices

### Typical Communication
- Quality and test plan reviews with Developers
- Acceptance criteria validation with Product Managers
- Bug reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Pre-release readiness assessments

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on test coverage and quality standards
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Report quality risks and blockers to Project Managers and Sponsors
- Support Technical Leads in designing testable architecture

---

## Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are executive or senior stakeholder representatives who hold decision-making authority and provide resources for projects. They approve business cases, allocate budget and personnel, and serve as final escalation points for business-impacting issues.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project business cases and resource allocation
- Make go/no-go decisions at major project gates
- Provide executive air cover and remove organizational blockers
- Ensure alignment with corporate strategy and priorities
- Approve major scope or timeline changes
- Receive and act on escalations for critical issues
- Support post-release announcements and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Reduce time to value for strategic initiatives
- Maintain organizational alignment and accountability
- Support team enablement and resource optimization

### Typical Communication
- Project approval and charter sign-offs
- Monthly or milestone-based executive status reports
- Escalation resolution meetings
- Post-release announcements and stakeholder briefings

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Receive project recommendations from Product Leads
- Review milestone updates from Project Managers
- Make final decisions on critical blockers and scope trade-offs
- Receive post-project retrospective summaries and learnings

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical guidance, design reviews, and decision-making for complex or cross-cutting technical concerns. They help identify technical risks, mentor developers, and ensure architectural coherence across initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct technical design reviews and provide architecture guidance
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Support estimation of technical complexity
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs
- Champion testing, observability, and maintainability
- Participate in capacity planning and dependency management

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable, and reliable systems
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team capability growth through mentoring
- Ensure informed technical decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review sessions
- Architecture documentation and decision logs
- Technical risk escalations to Project Managers
- Code review guidance and technical mentoring

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Guide Developers on architecture and design decisions
- Work with Product Managers to assess technical feasibility of requirements
- Support Project Managers in identifying technical dependencies and risks
- Collaborate with QA/Testing Leads on testability and observability design

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters (or Agile Coaches) facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and help teams maintain a healthy delivery rhythm. They support the team in adopting agile practices and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospective meetings
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Track team velocity and help manage work-in-progress
- Coach team members on agile practices and ceremonies
- Support capacity planning and sprint planning activities
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration
- Track action items and improvements from retrospectives

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable and predictable delivery pace
- Enable team self-organization and continuous improvement
- Reduce cycle time and improve flow
- Build team cohesion and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation
- Sprint planning and retrospective notes
- Blocker escalations to Project Managers
- Team health and velocity metrics

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work closely with Project Managers on schedule and milestone management
- Support Developers in removing impediments and maintaining flow
- Escalate team-level blockers to Project Managers
- Partner with Product Managers on backlog readiness and prioritization
